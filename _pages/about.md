I am a Ph.D. student at Cornell Information Science in the [Culture and Computation Lab](https://c2l.infosci.cornell.edu/), advised by [Matthew Wilkens](https://infosci.cornell.edu/content/wilkens). I approach digital humanities from two angles: the method of computational text analysis and the object of digital texts. I am specifically interested in Chinese and global queer web fictions.
