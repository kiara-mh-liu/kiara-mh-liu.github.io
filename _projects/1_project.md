This summer, I have been awarded with the [Siegel PiTech PhD Impact Fellowship](https://www.pi.tech.cornell.edu/pitech-phd-impact-fellowship). I am working with NYC's [Office of Technology and Innovation](https://www.nyc.gov/content/oti/pages/) to research machine translation quality evaluation (MTQE), with a specific focus on the City's use cases.

As a final deliverable for the project, I've written a blog post summarizing my work. You can find it [here]({% post_url 2025-09-30-pitech %}).
